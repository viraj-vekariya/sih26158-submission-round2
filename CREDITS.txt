Trimmed to the 60s close-orbit segment (48s-108s of the original) before processing.

3D model built with:
- VGGT (Visual Geometry Grounded Transformer), Meta, CVPR 2025 Best Paper
  github.com/facebookresearch/vggt -- camera pose + depth estimation
- Brush (Gaussian Splatting), Apache 2.0
  github.com/ArthurBrussee/brush -- photo-real 3D training

Both run entirely locally (Apple M4, no NVIDIA GPU, no cloud).
